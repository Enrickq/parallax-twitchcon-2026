THE EXPERIENCE / FIVE MOVEMENTS

Don't watch the future.
Make it.

Two possible futures. One community. A world that carries your choices into what happens next.

01 / SPLIT

Open the rift.

The streamer opens a round. Two futures appear and the countdown begins.

02 / CHOOSE

Leave your mark.

Type !a or !b in Twitch chat to choose a future. Send !spark hope to contribute a word to its visual DNA. One vote and one spark per username, each round.

03 / COLLAPSE

Make one real.

When time runs out, the winning branch changes the shared world. The streamer can also collapse early. Branch A wins a tie.

04 / WONDER

Visit the unlived future.

The Butterfly Lens shows both consequences from the same starting world. Enter a clearly labeled what-if preview. Your community's world and stream stay unchanged.

05 / REMEMBER

Carry the story forward.

Return to the present and open the next round. Harmony, memory and the visual genome carry forward. Revisit the latest twelve decisions in the archive.
